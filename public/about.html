About CeCoSUNK

CeCoSUNK is a celestial physics simulator made to run in your browser. It was made by Lawrence Tong, Nathaniel Shou, Ayush Raj, and others on Github.

Why CeCoSUNK?

CeCoSUNK is the fastest and most scientifically accurate* celestial physics simulator available on your browser. All calculations are performed in real time locally.

*Based on private benchmarks
